## *Security*

*Security tab facilitates access to the URLs and login credentials, enabling users to seamlessly log in to the Mendix Studio Pro and the Mendix Debugger.*

To access the `Security` tab, follow the steps below:

1. From the home page, choose one of the applications from the list. 
2. Once the application is selected, select one of the environments such as `Test`,`Acceptance` or `Production`.

![](../assets/environments_overview.jpg)

3. In the left-side menu, select `Security`.

![](../assets/security.jpg)

4. Under the `Admin User` section, you will find the URL and login credentials to log in to Mendix Studio Pro.

![](../assets/admin_user.jpg)

5. Under the `Debugging` section, you will find URL and password to login to the Mendix Debugger via Mendix Studio Pro.

![](../assets/debugger.jpg)
