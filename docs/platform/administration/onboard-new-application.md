This tutorial shows how to onboard a new application. 

**To login to the low-ops portal, follow the steps below:**

- Navigate to the link: https://portal.trial.low-ops.com/

- Click on the button `Log in with SSO`.

![](../../assets/login_platform.jpg)

- Enter your username/email and password.

![](../../assets/login_credentials.jpg)

- Click on the `Log in` button.

Once logged in, you will be redirected to the Low-Ops portal.

![](../../assets/low_ops_portal.jpg)

## Create New Application

**To create a new application, follow the steps below:**

- On the home page, click on the `Create app` buton. 

![](../../assets/create_new_application.jpg)

- In the pop up window, fill in the fields for the `App name` and `App tenant` and click on the `Create app` button. 

![](../../assets/create_new_component.jpg)

> **_NOTE:_** Allow a couple of minutes for the application to create. 

![](../../assets/component_creating.jpg)

- Once the application is created, it will appear in the list of the apps on the home page. 

![](../../assets/component_created.jpg)
